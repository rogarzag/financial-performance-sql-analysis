# Financial Performance Analysis with SQL

SQL analysis of revenue, costs, gross profit, margins, and marketing ROI across six countries to support financial and commercial decision-making.

## Business question

How does financial performance vary across countries, and where do revenue, margin, and marketing efficiency indicate stronger or weaker commercial performance?

## Approach

The analysis compared performance across six countries using SQL-based aggregation and KPI calculations. It focused on:

- Revenue
- Costs
- Gross profit
- Gross margin
- Marketing ROI
- Country-level performance comparisons

The goal was to identify differences in financial and marketing efficiency that could support commercial prioritization.

## Key findings

- The **United States** showed the strongest overall performance.
- **Canada** and the **United Kingdom** showed lower marketing efficiency relative to the other markets analyzed.
- Reviewing revenue alone was not sufficient; margin and marketing ROI provided additional context for evaluating market performance.

## Business recommendations

- Prioritize country-level performance reviews using revenue, gross profit, margin, and marketing ROI together.
- Investigate the drivers behind weaker marketing efficiency in Canada and the United Kingdom.
- Use country comparisons to support budget allocation and commercial prioritization.
- Monitor whether higher revenue markets also sustain stronger margins and marketing returns over time.
